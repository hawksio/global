How codes are protected

  • 6-digit codes from a cryptographically secure generator, stored only as keyed hashes.
  • Single use, 10–30 minute expiry, 5 attempts, 60-second resend cooldown, rate-limited.
  • Email bodies are never stored or logged, so administrators never see codes or reset links.
  • Answers never reveal whether an address has an account.
