Tips for reaching the inbox
  • Write like a person: a short, specific subject and a few plain sentences. No ALL CAPS, no “FREE!!!”.
  • Keep links to one or two, and no attachments or big images in first emails.
  • Email people who will recognise why you're writing. Fewer, better-matched people beats a big list.
  • Start small with a new domain (20–50 a day) and grow over a few weeks.
  • Your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC are checked automatically; every email has an unsubscribe link and no tracking.
  • No sending tool can guarantee the inbox. Replies and low spam reports are what build your reputation.
