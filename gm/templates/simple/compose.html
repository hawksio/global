Tips for reaching the inbox
  • Write like a person: a short, specific subject and a few plain sentences. No ALL CAPS, no “FREE!!!”.
  • Keep links to one or two, and no attachments or big images in first emails.
  • Email people who will recognise why you're writing. Fewer, better-matched people beats a big list.
  • Start small with a new domain (20–50 a day) and grow over a few weeks. Use “Gently” for big lists.
  • Your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC are checked automatically. Emails go out as plain text with no tracking, and each one says people can reply to opt out. If someone does, click “Asked to stop” next to their name in Sent.
  • Gmail's Promotions tab isn't spam: it's where Gmail puts mail that looks like marketing. Plain, short, personal emails without offers or prices are the most likely to reach Primary. When people reply, or move your email to Primary, Gmail learns.
  • No sending tool can guarantee the inbox. Replies and low spam reports are what build your reputation.
