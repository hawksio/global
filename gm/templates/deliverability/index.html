Setup guide

  1. In Resend, add your domain; a sending subdomain such as news.yourcompany.com keeps marketing reputation separate. Resend: domains
  2. Copy the DNS records Resend shows (SPF and MX on send., DKIM at resend._domainkey) into your DNS host (for cPanel: Zone Editor).
  3. Add a DMARC record at _dmarc, starting with p=none and a report address. Resend: DMARC
  4. Wait for DNS to update (minutes to hours), then run the checks above and verify the domain in Resend.
  5. Start small: send to your most engaged, recently verified contacts first and grow volume gradually.
  6. Follow the mailbox providers' sender rules: Google · Yahoo.
